Viva Questions
What is primary structure?
Amino acid sequence of a protein.
What is secondary structure?
Local folding such as alpha helices and beta sheets.
What is tertiary structure?
Complete 3D folding of a single protein chain.
What is quaternary structure?
Association of multiple protein subunits.
What is a protein domain?
Functional and structural unit within a protein.
What is a motif?
Small conserved sequence pattern.
What is AlphaFold?
AI-based system for predicting protein structures.
What is RMSD?
Measure of structural similarity between proteins.
What is a Ramachandran Plot?
Graph showing allowed phi and psi backbone angles.
